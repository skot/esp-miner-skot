BM1372/BM1373 provides four multiplexed internal supply-sense channels per ASIC, distinct from individual cores and the eight hashrate counter domains. S23-hyd FR-1.29 `cminer` (SHA-256 `d4907d59ec5fabf926b2a11985a090f23075ba937e2bab1c468568c49498e805`) function `0x513c8` selects ADC inputs 1, 2, 4, 5 for channels 0–3. It writes B8=`0x230C000D | (selector << 13)`, B9=`0x3F014081`, BA=`0x00040010`, BB=`0x03340E80`, then ten times writes B8=`0x232C000D | (selector << 13)`, waits 60 ms, and reads BD. Discard the first sample and average the remaining nine DV0_DOUT values (bits 14:0); DV1_DOUT is unused. Millivolts are `k * 600 * (mean / 16384 - 1)`, with k=1 for channels 0–1 and k=2 for channels 2–3. ELF literals and ARM VNMLS at `0x51724–0x517a4` confirm the subtraction direction. Stock serializes this ADC operation with die-temperature sampling and performs no stop/restore writes afterward. ESP-Miner uses the same sampling sequence, checks BD.DATA_RDY (bit 31), and samples one channel per one-second monitor poll, refreshing all four about every four seconds; missing replies, failed setup, power-down, reset, and ten-second-old values are unavailable. `/api/system/info` and live telemetry expose `asicDomainVoltages` in millivolts as ASIC-indexed arrays of four nullable channels, or an empty array on unsupported models. These readings do not change voltage or cooling control. Physical assignment to individual cores and absolute calibration remain unverified.
