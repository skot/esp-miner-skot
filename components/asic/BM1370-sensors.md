BM1370 telemetry follows S23-hyd FR-1.29 `cminer` (SHA-256 `d4907d59ec5fabf926b2a11985a090f23075ba937e2bab1c468568c49498e805`), BM1370 driver functions `0x4bf4c` (temperature) and `0x4b914` (voltage). Temperature shares the voltage ADC: phase A initializes B8=`238c000d`, BA=`00040010`, BB=`03340e80`, waits 20 ms, then 21 times writes B8=`23ac000d`, B9=`3f4141af` and reads BD; discard the first and average the remaining 20 DV0 values (low 15 bits). Phase B initializes B8=`238ce00d`, B9=`3f0141af`, BA=`00040010`, BB=`03340e80`, waits 20 ms, then 21 times writes B8=`23ace00d`, B9=`3f4141af` and reads BD; discard the first and average the remaining 20 DV1 values (bits 30:16). Preserve stock integer arithmetic: `a=sumDV0/20`, `b=sumDV1/20`, `code=a+16384-((a+b)>>1)`, `C=code*0.0309-769.25`; literals at `0x4c490/0x4c498` and VNMLS at `0x4c3ea` confirm this conversion. Voltage selects mux inputs 1,2,4,5 with B8=`230c030d|(selector<<13)`, B9=`3f014381`, BA=`00040010`, BB=`03340e80`; ten times write B8=`232c030d|(selector<<13)`, wait 60 ms and read BD. Discard the first and average nine DV0 values: `mV=k*1000*(mean/16384-1)`, k=1 for channels 1/2 and k=2 for 3/4. BM1370 does not use BM1372's 0.6 gain. Stock serializes both complete acquisitions with the same mutex and does not restore the ADC afterward. ESP-Miner acquires one full temperature measurement or one voltage channel per monitor poll, refreshing everything about every five seconds, checks BD.DATA_RDY (bit31) on retained voltage samples, and rejects missing replies and incomplete writes. On the attached Gamma, automatic temperature conversions return nonzero data with DATA_RDY clear (phase A example `0x172f6ade`); like stock, temperature sampling consumes these replies without requiring DATA_RDY. Cache entries expire after ten seconds and reset on ASIC initialization; API values are null while ASIC power is disabled. Existing `asicDieTemps` and `asicDomainVoltages` API fields and AxeOS Heat/Power cards display these readings. Cooling and voltage control continue using their existing sensors. Absolute calibration and physical mapping of the four internal supply-sense channels remain unverified.

The stock temperature loops have no explicit sleep, but their synchronous read adapter (`0x34b44`, called via `0x7286c` → `0x72258` → `0x1ec3c`) sleeps 5 ms at `0x34c8c` after sending each read, before consuming its response. Direct UART acquisition without this pacing returned data from the preceding mux selection on the Gamma; ESP-Miner waits 10 ms between the temperature control writes and each BD read, preserving the stock minimum spacing with a margin. A full two-phase temperature acquisition takes about half a second and remains serialized with voltage sampling.
