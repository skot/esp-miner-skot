BM1372/BM1373 on-die temperature support follows the S23-hyd FR-1.29 `cminer` binary (SHA-256 `d4907d59ec5fabf926b2a11985a090f23075ba937e2bab1c468568c49498e805`), functions `0x52b60` and `0x52c40`. Register `0xb2` controls the sensor; preserve all bits outside `0xb1020000`, then write masked values `0x00020000`, `0x80020000`, `0x81020000`, `0x90020000`, waiting 10 ms after each stage before reading `0xb3`. Low 16 bits are DOUT; the conversion is `DOUT * 683.254 / 4096 - 289.18` (1884 gives 25.09015 °C, 2333 gives 99.98787 °C). The existing `re-s23hyd` thermal report has the subtraction reversed: ARM `VNMLS` at `0x52ebe` computes product minus the previous destination; see [ARM's instruction definition](https://support.arm.com/documentation/100235/0002/the-cortex-m33-instruction-set/floating-point-instructions/vnmla--vnmls-and-vnmul). ESP-Miner checks TS_DONE (bit 31) and TS_ERROR (bit 30), leaves DOUT_TYPE (bit 29) unconstrained, and expires readings after five seconds; stock only consumes DOUT. `/api/system/info` and live telemetry expose `asicDieTemps` in chain order, with null for unavailable readings and an empty array on other ASIC models. These readings supplement the existing external-diode temperatures and do not control cooling. Absolute accuracy and status-bit behavior still require validation on Naja hardware.
