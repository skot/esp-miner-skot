BM1372/BM1373 on-die temperature support follows the S23-hyd FR-1.29 `cminer` binary (SHA-256 `d4907d59ec5fabf926b2a11985a090f23075ba937e2bab1c468568c49498e805`), functions `0x52b60` and `0x52c40`. Control register `0xb2` is write-only: use the stock register shadow with TS_CLK_EN set, writing `0x40020000`, `0x60020000`, `0x61020000`, `0x70020000`, waiting 10 ms after each stage before reading `0xb3`. TS_PD stays zero; RUN_CTRL is enabled in the second stage, CLOAD pulses in the third, and RUN_EN is enabled in the fourth. The setter pairs the TS_PD string at `0x1df750` with constant zero and RUN_CTRL at `0x1df744` with the stack argument; the existing reverse-engineering report swapped these fields. Temperature access uses each assigned chip address (0x00 and 0x10 on the two-chip Naja), matching its response address. Low 16 bits are DOUT; the conversion is `DOUT * 683.254 / 4096 - 289.18` (1884 gives 25.09015 °C, 2333 gives 99.98787 °C). The existing report also reverses this subtraction: ARM VNMLS at `0x52ebe` computes product minus the previous destination; see [ARM's instruction definition](https://support.arm.com/documentation/100235/0002/the-cortex-m33-instruction-set/floating-point-instructions/vnmla--vnmls-and-vnmul). ESP-Miner checks TS_DONE (bit 31) and TS_ERROR (bit 30), leaves DOUT_TYPE (bit 29) unconstrained, logs each chip's first raw result, and expires readings after five seconds; stock only consumes DOUT. `/api/system/info` and live telemetry expose `asicDieTemps` in chain order, with null for unavailable readings and an empty array on other ASIC models. These readings supplement the existing external-diode temperatures and do not control cooling. Absolute accuracy requires comparison with a calibrated reference.
